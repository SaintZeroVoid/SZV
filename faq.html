Frequently Asked Questions

What is a generic medicine?

Generic medicines are drugs that have the same active ingredients, strength, dosage form, and route of administration as brand-name drugs, but are typically sold at lower prices.

How do I place an order?

You can place an order by browsing our shop, adding products to your cart, and proceeding to checkout. For wholesale orders, please use the trading page to submit an inquiry.

Do you offer delivery services?

Yes, we offer delivery services for both retail and wholesale orders. Delivery times and fees may vary depending on your location.

Can I return or exchange products?

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 14 days of purchase, provided the products are unopened and in their original packaging. Please contact our support team for assistance.

How can I contact customer support?

You can contact our customer support team via the contact page, email, or phone. We are here to help with any questions or concerns you may have.
